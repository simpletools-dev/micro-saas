Privacy Policy

Nickolas Video Uploader is a private tool used solely by its owner to upload videos to his own YouTube channels.

Data collected

The app accesses your YouTube channel information and uploads videos you choose, using Google OAuth. No data is stored on any server, shared with third parties, or used for any other purpose.

Data use

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and used only to authenticate YouTube uploads. Nothing is transmitted anywhere except to Google's YouTube API for the upload itself.
