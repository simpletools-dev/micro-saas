American AI labs

The four US labs tracked here are building frontier AI systems on American infrastructure. Careers links go to each company's official careers page, verified October 16, 2026.

OpenAI

Headquarters: San Francisco, California

Leads the $500B Stargate joint venture with SoftBank and Oracle, targeting 10 GW of US AI compute. The Abilene, Texas flagship (1.2 GW, NVIDIA GB200) is partially operational, with additional US campuses in development in Texas, New Mexico, Ohio, Michigan, and Wisconsin.

Sources: Intuition Labs, Data Center Dynamics

View open roles at OpenAI →

Anthropic

Headquarters: San Francisco, California

Primary compute partner is AWS, whose $11B Project Rainier campus in New Carlisle, Indiana hosts nearly 500,000 custom Trainium2 chips for training Claude models. Anthropic also leases large GPU capacity on SpaceXAI's Colossus cluster in Memphis, Tennessee.

Sources: i10x.ai, Neowin, Zubiqo

SpaceXAI (formerly xAI)

Headquarters: Palo Alto, California

Acquired by SpaceX in February 2026 and rebranded SpaceXAI in July 2026. Operates the Colossus supercomputer cluster in Memphis, Tennessee and Southaven, Mississippi, targeting about 2 GW of compute with over a million NVIDIA GPUs planned by end of 2026, and leases capacity to Anthropic and Google.

Sources: Wikipedia: SpaceXAI, Wikipedia: Colossus (data center)

View open roles at SpaceXAI →

Google DeepMind

Headquarters: London, United Kingdom (with Mountain View, California via Google)

Gemini models run on Google's US AI infrastructure, including the Columbus, Ohio and Omaha, Nebraska / Council Bluffs, Iowa TPU clusters, a $4B campus under construction in West Memphis, Arkansas, and a $40B Texas investment program that includes new air-cooled data center capacity.

Sources: Comparative AI (GitHub), When In Your State, Neowin
