OpenAI

Headquarters: San Francisco, California

Leads the $500B Stargate joint venture with SoftBank and Oracle, targeting 10 GW of US AI compute. The Abilene, Texas flagship (1.2 GW, NVIDIA GB200) is partially operational, with additional US campuses in development in Texas, New Mexico, Ohio, Michigan, and Wisconsin.

OpenAI leads the Stargate joint venture with SoftBank and Oracle: a $500 billion program targeting 10 GW of US AI compute. The flagship campus in Abilene, Texas is being developed by Crusoe and leased to Oracle and OpenAI. Eight buildings are planned on about 4 million square feet with 1.2 GW of total power capacity. Construction has run since July 2024, and the first NVIDIA GB200 racks arrived earlier this year. The first two buildings, 980,000 square feet supporting 200MW+ of IT load, are operational and used by Oracle Cloud Infrastructure for OpenAI. Crusoe says about 7,000 electricians, construction workers, and skilled tradespeople come to the site every day. The second phase, six more buildings, is expected to complete in mid-2026. Additional Stargate sites are in development in Shackelford County and Milam County in Texas, Lordstown in Ohio, and Doña Ana County in New Mexico.

Why it matters

Stargate is the largest announced AI infrastructure program in the US. Its 10 GW target and $500 billion scope make OpenAI the lab whose buildout most directly tests whether the country can deliver power and construction at this scale.

Sources: Intuition Labs, Data Center Dynamics

Anthropic

Headquarters: San Francisco, California

Primary compute partner is AWS, whose $11B Project Rainier campus in New Carlisle, Indiana hosts nearly 500,000 custom Trainium2 chips for training Claude models. Anthropic also leases large GPU capacity on SpaceXAI's Colossus cluster in Memphis, Tennessee.

Anthropic's primary compute partner is AWS. The anchor is Project Rainier, an $11 billion gigawatt-scale AI campus in New Carlisle, Indiana, now operational and built around nearly 500,000 of AWS's custom Trainium2 chips, with plans to scale beyond one million. It trains and deploys Anthropic's Claude models outside the NVIDIA-dominated GPU market; Amazon has invested $4 billion in Anthropic, and the campus is the physical form of that partnership. Anthropic also leases large GPU capacity on SpaceXAI's Colossus cluster in Memphis: in May 2026 it agreed to rent essentially all compute capacity at Colossus 1.

Why it matters

Project Rainier is the clearest example of custom-silicon strategy: nearly 500,000 Trainium2 chips purpose-built for one lab's models. If it works, it proves frontier AI can be trained outside NVIDIA's ecosystem.

Sources: i10x.ai, Neowin, Zubiqo

SpaceXAI (formerly xAI)

Headquarters: Palo Alto, California

Acquired by SpaceX in February 2026 and rebranded SpaceXAI in July 2026. Operates the Colossus supercomputer cluster in Memphis, Tennessee and Southaven, Mississippi, targeting about 2 GW of compute with over a million NVIDIA GPUs planned by end of 2026, and leases capacity to Anthropic and Google.

SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026 and rebranded the division SpaceXAI in July 2026. On October 4, 2026, Elon Musk confirmed it would be renamed SpaceXSI, the first major AI company to adopt the federal SI vocabulary. The company's US footprint is the Colossus cluster across Memphis, Tennessee and Southaven, Mississippi, targeting about 2 GW of compute. Colossus 1 was built at a former Electrolux plant in South Memphis: roughly 100,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs went live in mid-to-late 2024 after a 122-day build, later joined by H200 and early B200 systems. Colossus 2, branded Macrohard, sits at 5420 Tulane Road in Whitehaven; its first major cluster of about 110,000 GB200-class processors and roughly 210 MW came online around January 2026 after a reported 91-day build. By late September 2026, 440,000 GB300 GPUs were in use with 660,000 more expected by the end of 2026. Power comes from behind-the-meter gas turbines in Southaven plus a permanent roughly 1.2 GW plant permitted in March 2026, with temporary units scheduled for removal by mid-2027. The cluster also serves outside customers: Google agreed in June 2026 to rent about 110,000 GPUs for $920 million per month from October 2026 through June 2029.

Why it matters

Colossus shows the speed extreme of the buildout: 100,000 GPUs live in 122 days, a second cluster in about 91 days. It also shows the power constraint, with gas turbines filling the gap while permanent generation is built.

Sources: Wikipedia: SpaceXAI, Wikipedia: Colossus (data center)

Google DeepMind

Headquarters: London, United Kingdom (with Mountain View, California via Google)

Gemini models run on Google's US AI infrastructure, including the Columbus, Ohio and Omaha, Nebraska / Council Bluffs, Iowa TPU clusters, a $4B campus under construction in West Memphis, Arkansas, and a $40B Texas investment program that includes new air-cooled data center capacity.

DeepMind is headquartered in London with Mountain View, California as its US base through Google. Its Gemini models run on Google's US AI infrastructure: TPU clusters in Columbus, Ohio and Omaha, Nebraska / Council Bluffs, Iowa, a $4 billion campus under construction in West Memphis, Arkansas since October 2025 on about 1,100 acres, and a $40 billion Texas investment program that includes new air-cooled data center capacity.

Why it matters

DeepMind's US footprint is Google's infrastructure at national scale: TPU clusters across three states plus a $4 billion Arkansas campus. It is the quietest buildout of the four, and one of the largest.

Sources: Comparative AI (GitHub), When In Your State, Neowin

Questions

Labs FAQ

Answers grounded in the sourced profiles above.

Which lab has the largest US buildout?

By announced program value and capacity target, OpenAI: the Stargate joint venture is a $500 billion program targeting 10 GW of US AI compute. By operating hardware, SpaceXAI's Colossus cluster was on the order of 1 GW of AI compute capacity as of mid-2026, targeting about 2 GW. Both figures are announced or reported amounts, not audited totals.

Do all four labs train models on US infrastructure?

Yes, per the tracked buildouts. OpenAI on Stargate's US campuses, Anthropic on AWS's Project Rainier in Indiana and leased Colossus capacity in Memphis, SpaceXAI on Colossus in Memphis and Southaven, and Google DeepMind on Google's US TPU and data center infrastructure.

Are these labs hiring?

Each profile below links to the company's official careers page, verified live on October 16, 2026. Those pages list current openings across research, engineering, and operations.

What is the White House Accord on Super Intelligence?

A voluntary industry commitment on AI safety controls and incident reporting, signed on September 29, 2026 by the chief executives of Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI, and Nvidia. It creates no legally enforceable obligations. See the policy timeline.

Why is one lab called SpaceXAI (formerly xAI)?

SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026 and rebranded the division SpaceXAI in July 2026. On October 4, 2026, Elon Musk confirmed a further rename to SpaceXSI. The page uses the name in effect when the data was compiled.
