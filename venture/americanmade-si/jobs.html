SI-economy jobs

The US superintelligence buildout is a jobs story before it is anything else: concrete, electrical, technicians, engineers, and operations. This page describes the kinds of work the tracked projects create, with real reported figures and links to the companies' official careers pages. These are role categories and project data, not job listings. No salary figures are shown because pay varies by role, location, and employer. Careers links were verified October 16, 2026.

Real example: when we checked xAI's careers page on October 16, 2026, its featured roles included Construction Scheduler, Controls Technician, Plumbing Technician, and Fire Protection Technician in the Memphis area, alongside engineering roles. Buildouts hire trades as well as researchers.

Why it matters

Most coverage of the AI race is about models and chips. The employment story is different: thousands of construction and trades jobs arrive years before any server does, and permanent operations jobs are far fewer than peak construction jobs. Reading the buildout as a labor story means watching which projects are under construction now, because that is where the hiring is.

Job categories

Five categories of SI-economy work

Each category below names the tracked projects where that work is happening.

Construction

General construction, site preparation, concrete, steel, and project scheduling on new campuses. Data center builds need crews long before any server arrives. Projects currently under construction in the tracked table include Canton and Ridgeland in Mississippi, Richland Parish in Louisiana, Cheyenne in Wyoming, Vaucluse in South Carolina, West Memphis in Arkansas, and the Licking County expansion in Ohio. Crusoe says about 7,000 electricians, construction workers, and skilled tradespeople come to the Stargate campus in Abilene, Texas every day.

Electricians

Electrical distribution, power systems, and high-voltage work at data center and fab sites. The buildout is fundamentally a power story: Abilene is planned for 1.2 GW, Hyperion for 5 GW, Colossus for about 2 GW. New generation is being built alongside the campuses, including a roughly 1.2 GW gas plant for Colossus in Southaven, Mississippi and Entergy plants for Hyperion in Louisiana.

Technicians

Controls, plumbing, fire protection, and data center technicians who install and maintain facility systems. When we checked xAI's careers page on October 16, 2026, its featured roles included Construction Scheduler, Controls Technician, Plumbing Technician, and Fire Protection Technician in the Memphis area, alongside engineering roles. Operating campuses that need technicians now include Abilene, Memphis, New Carlisle, Jeffersonville, New Albany, and Kuna.

Engineers

AI research, software, systems, and hardware engineers at the labs building the models. The four tracked labs, OpenAI, Anthropic, SpaceXAI, and Google DeepMind, all hire engineers across research and infrastructure. AWS's Project Rainier alone runs nearly 500,000 Trainium2 chips for Anthropic, which is a systems engineering operation at national scale.

Operations

Facilities operations, logistics, and program operations that keep campuses running day to day. Meta says its Hyperion campus will support 1,000 permanent jobs once operational; SK hynix expects around 1,000 facility jobs at its Indiana packaging plant; Samsung expects 1,800 permanent jobs at Taylor, Texas within a decade. These are company projections, not current openings.

Where to look first

Match the project phase to the role

Hiring follows construction phases. Use the buildout table status column as your guide:

  • In development or announced (Shackelford County, Milam County, Lordstown, Doña Ana County, Southaven, El Paso): planning and permitting roles; construction hiring comes later. Watch these for the next wave.
  • Under construction (Canton, Ridgeland, Richland Parish, Cheyenne, Vaucluse, West Memphis, Licking County, Abilene phase 2): construction, electrical, and project scheduling roles are active now.
  • Operating (Abilene phase 1, Memphis, New Carlisle, New Albany, Jeffersonville, Kuna): technicians, facilities, and operations roles. The labs hire research and engineering continuously regardless of phase.

How to use this

Working the careers pages

Company careers pages are the primary source for openings; this site does not intermediate. A practical approach:

  • Match the project phase to the role. Campuses under construction hire construction, electrical, and project roles. Operating campuses hire technicians and operations. Labs hire research and engineering year-round.
  • Search by project city. The buildout table gives the city for every project; use it as the location filter on the careers page.
  • Check trades roles, not just engineering. The verified xAI Memphis snapshot shows construction scheduler, controls, plumbing, and fire protection roles featured alongside engineering.
  • Expect change. Openings fill and pages update. The links below were verified on October 16, 2026; what you find today reflects current hiring.

Questions

Jobs FAQ

Is this a job board with current listings?

No. This page describes the kinds of work the buildout creates and where, with links to the companies' official careers pages. We do not aggregate or republish job listings. For current openings, follow the careers links, which were verified on October 16, 2026.

Why do you not show salary figures?

Pay varies by role, employer, location, and experience, and we do not have verified compensation data for these projects. Rather than publish unverified numbers, we show none.

Which sites are hiring trades workers right now?

Construction and trades hiring follows construction activity. Projects listed as under construction in the buildout table, including Canton and Ridgeland in Mississippi, Richland Parish in Louisiana, and the Stargate second phase in Abilene, Texas, are the active sites. Crusoe reported about 7,000 tradespeople on site daily at Abilene.

How current are the careers links?

All four were verified live on October 16, 2026. Company careers pages change as openings fill, so treat them as starting points and check the pages directly.

Do the labs only hire researchers?

No. When we checked xAI's careers page on October 16, 2026, featured Memphis-area roles included Construction Scheduler, Controls Technician, Plumbing Technician, and Fire Protection Technician alongside engineering roles. The buildout hires trades as well as researchers.
