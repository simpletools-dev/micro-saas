Why this site exists

Three tracks, one story

The superintelligence story in America runs on three tracks at once. Most coverage follows one of them. We track all three, because the policy only makes sense next to the concrete and the megawatts.

Track 1

The policy track

In September and October 2026, Washington moved fast: a presidential speech at the UN General Assembly floated renaming artificial intelligence, Executive Order 14434 made "Super Intelligence" the executive branch term of record, the chief executives of Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI, and Nvidia signed a voluntary White House Accord on Super Intelligence, and the Super Intelligence Force was created with 120 days to report back.

Follow the policy timeline →
Track 2

The build track

While Washington renamed the technology, American companies kept building it. OpenAI, SoftBank, and Oracle are assembling the $500 billion Stargate program toward 10 GW of US AI compute. SpaceXAI runs the Colossus cluster in Memphis and Southaven. AWS built Project Rainier, an $11 billion Indiana campus, for Anthropic. Five chip fab projects from TSMC, Intel, Samsung, Micron, and SK hynix are adding US semiconductor capacity.

Follow the buildout →
Track 3

The jobs track

The buildout is a construction boom before it is anything else. About 7,000 electricians, construction workers, and skilled tradespeople show up daily at the Stargate campus in Abilene, Texas. Meta says its Hyperion campus in Louisiana will employ around 7,500 workers at peak construction. Samsung reported its Taylor, Texas campus supported 8,868 direct and 9,768 indirect construction jobs in 2024.
