Latest developments

The three newest items in the federal SI policy timeline.

October 15, 2026

SIF holds its first meeting

The Super Intelligence Force was scheduled to hold its first meeting on October 15, 2026, with leadership expected to publish goals and responsibilities afterward.

Read the full timeline →
October 6, 2026

Justice Department adopts SI terminology in staff memo

Acting Deputy Attorney General Trent McCotter issued a Justice Department memo directing staff to use 'super intelligence' and 'SI' instead of 'AI' in official communications and, where appropriate, court filings, implementing the September executive order.

Read the full timeline →
October 4, 2026

Super Intelligence Force (SIF) announced

President Trump announced the Super Intelligence Force (SIF) in a Truth Social post, a federal task force chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton with three vice chairs, given 120 days to report on AI risks and opportunities and the federal government's role, implying a report around early February 2027.
