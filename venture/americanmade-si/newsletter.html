The SI buildout, in your inbox

Two dates are coming that this site exists to cover: the proposed federal SI definition due November 28, 2026, and the Super Intelligence Force report due around early February 2027. Sign up and you will hear about both, plus what the US buildout adds each month.

What you get

  • The SIF report alert: one email the week the Super Intelligence Force report drops (expected around early February 2027, approximate), with what it says and what it means.
  • Monthly buildout recaps: new labs, fabs, data center milestones, and policy moves, each with source links, in one short email.

Who this is for

  • Job seekers watching where the buildout is hiring, from construction trades to lab engineering.

What a monthly recap looks like

Five or six short items, each with a source link: what changed, where, and what it means. New data center milestones, fab production starts, lab announcements, and policy moves. No hot takes, no filler, no sponsored content. If a month is quiet, the email says so and stays short.

What you do not get
