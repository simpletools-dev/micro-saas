About American-Made Superintelligence

American-Made Superintelligence ("Superintelligence, made in America") is an independent tracker of the US superintelligence race: American AI labs, US chip fabs, data center buildouts, and the federal SI policy timeline.

Methodology

  • All factual claims come from public reporting, with the source linked next to each claim.
  • Data was compiled and accessed on October 16, 2026.
  • Investment figures and capacities are announced or reported amounts, not audited totals.
  • Dates marked approximate (such as the SIF report date) are labeled as approximate on the page.
  • Careers links point to official company careers pages, verified live on October 16, 2026.

Limitations

  • Announced projects are not completed projects. Timelines slip, scopes change, and some projects stall.
  • Data changes fast in this sector. Figures here reflect reporting available on October 16, 2026.
  • We track what is reported. We do not independently verify company announcements.
  • This site is not affiliated with the US government or any company mentioned.
