Federal SI policy timeline

Every event below comes from public reporting, with sources linked. Summaries are neutral and factual.

Note on dates: the Super Intelligence Force was given 120 days to report starting October 4, 2026, which implies a report around early February 2027. That date is approximate, not confirmed.
October 15, 2026

SIF holds its first meeting

The Super Intelligence Force was scheduled to hold its first meeting on October 15, 2026, with leadership expected to publish goals and responsibilities afterward.

Sources: Govt Schemes

October 6, 2026

Justice Department adopts SI terminology in staff memo

Acting Deputy Attorney General Trent McCotter issued a Justice Department memo directing staff to use 'super intelligence' and 'SI' instead of 'AI' in official communications and, where appropriate, court filings, implementing the September executive order.

Sources: Analytics Insight

October 4, 2026

Super Intelligence Force (SIF) announced

President Trump announced the Super Intelligence Force (SIF) in a Truth Social post, a federal task force chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton with three vice chairs, given 120 days to report on AI risks and opportunities and the federal government's role, implying a report around early February 2027.

Sources: PYMNTS, Oossa

October 4, 2026

Musk says SpaceXAI will be renamed SpaceXSI

Elon Musk confirmed that SpaceX's AI arm, SpaceXAI, will be renamed SpaceXSI, following the September executive order replacing 'AI' with 'SI' in executive branch usage.

Sources: The Hindu BusinessLine (ANI)

October 2, 2026

.si domain surge reported: 44,000 September registrations

Reporting from Register.si, Slovenia's domain registry, showed 44,000 new .si registrations in September 2026 versus under 2,000 in August, an increase of more than 2,100%, which the registry called unprecedented.

Sources: BBC via WeSearch, BizToc

September 29, 2026

White House Accord on Super Intelligence signed

Chief executives of Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI, and Nvidia signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, a voluntary industry commitment on AI safety controls and incident reporting.

Sources: Tech Times

September 29, 2026

Executive Order 14434 signed: 'Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence'

President Trump signed Executive Order 14434, 'Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence,' directing executive branch agencies to use 'Super Intelligence' and 'SI' in place of 'Artificial Intelligence' and 'AI' in official non-statutory communications, and tasking the science adviser with proposing a formal federal definition.

Sources: Federal Register, The Register

September 22, 2026

Trump floats AI rename at UN General Assembly

Speaking at the UN General Assembly, President Trump said he intended to rename artificial intelligence, arguing that the word 'artificial' makes the technology sound fake and that US government documents would use 'super intelligence' instead.

Sources: Lior AI (citing AP)
