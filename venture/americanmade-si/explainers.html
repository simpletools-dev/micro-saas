What the AI to SI rename means

On September 29, 2026, President Trump signed Executive Order 14434, titled "Inaugurating the Era of Super Intelligence." The order directs executive branch agencies to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in place of "Artificial Intelligence" and "AI" in official non-statutory communications. It also tasks the science adviser with proposing a formal federal definition.

  • Non-statutory matters: the order covers executive branch communications. It does not change laws passed by Congress, which would require legislation.
  • It is being implemented: on October 6, 2026, the Justice Department issued a staff memo directing employees to use "super intelligence" and "SI" in official communications and, where appropriate, court filings.
  • Industry is following along: the same week the order was signed, the chief executives of Google, Anthropic, Meta, OpenAI, xAI, and Nvidia signed the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, a voluntary industry commitment on safety controls and incident reporting. Elon Musk later confirmed SpaceX's AI arm would be renamed SpaceXSI.
  • The public noticed: .si domain registrations jumped from under 2,000 in August 2026 to 44,000 in September, an increase of more than 2,100 percent reported by Register.si via the BBC.

What the Super Intelligence Force does

On October 4, 2026, President Trump announced the Super Intelligence Force (SIF) in a Truth Social post. It is a federal task force chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, with three vice chairs.

  • Its job: the task force was given 120 days to report on AI risks and opportunities and the federal government's role.
  • When the report is due: 120 days from October 4, 2026 implies a report around early February 2027. That date is approximate, not officially confirmed.
  • First meeting: the SIF was scheduled to hold its first meeting on October 15, 2026, with leadership expected to publish goals and responsibilities afterward.
  • What to watch: whether the report lands on time, what it recommends, and which recommendations become policy. This site's newsletter sends one email when the report drops.
