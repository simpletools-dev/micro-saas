Key exclusions

  • Pre-existing conditions
  • Improper installations
  • Items outside the main home foundation (typical)

Waiting period

30 days standard (some sources report 30-60 days; your contract states the exact period); immediate for seller listing coverage

Cancellation policy

Within 30 days: full premium refund minus a $50 cancellation fee (and claims paid). After 30 days: pro-rated refund minus $50 fee and claims paid

Fine-print highlights

  • 40+ add-on riders - by far the biggest customization catalog (pools, pest control, gutter cleaning, re-keying).
  • 365-day workmanship guarantee - the longest of the six.
  • Multi-year plans earn a free month per year committed (3-year plan = 3 free months).
  • Caps are modest: $2,000/item, and plumbing/electrical are capped at just $500 each.
  • Service fee is not disclosed until late in the sign-up process - confirm yours before committing.
  • Available in all 50 states.
Plain-English verdict: Liberty is the pick if you want to tailor coverage precisely and keep the monthly cost moderate. Just know the $500 plumbing/electrical caps and $2,000/item ceiling make it a poor fit for homes with aging major systems.
