Fine-print highlights

  • Lowest monthly premiums of the six companies - often under $50/mo with discount.
  • Free limited roof-leak protection included on all plans (most competitors charge extra).
  • The trade-off: per-item caps are the lowest here. A $500/appliance cap will not cover a full refrigerator replacement.
  • No standard published service fee - yours is set at purchase, so get it in writing.
  • Add-ons start at $3.33/mo, among the cheapest.
Plain-English verdict: Select is the budget play: cheapest way to get a combo plan with roof-leak coverage thrown in. But the low caps mean it functions more like repair-cost assistance than real replacement coverage. Fine for newer homes; risky for aging HVAC and appliances.
