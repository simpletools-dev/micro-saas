Fine-print highlights

  • Covers unknown pre-existing conditions - like AHS, a rare and valuable inclusion.
  • 180-day workmanship guarantee - six times the industry-standard 30 days.
  • Repair + Replace plans include up to $500 reimbursement of your homeowners insurance deductible.
  • Lowest entry price of the six - but the $150 max service fee is the highest.
  • Low caps across the board ($1,500 HVAC, $1,000 plumbing, $10,000 aggregate) mean big failures still cost you.
  • Not available in Alaska or Hawaii.
Plain-English verdict: Cinch is the low-commitment starter warranty: cheap to try, generous workmanship guarantee, and it covers unknown pre-existing conditions. Treat it as repair assistance, not catastrophe insurance - the caps are too low for a full system replacement.
