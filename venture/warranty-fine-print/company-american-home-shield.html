Fine-print highlights

  • Covers unknown pre-existing conditions - rare in the industry and a big differentiator.
  • Highest aggregate cap of the six companies we track ($50,000/term).
  • ShieldPlatinum adds roof-leak repair (up to $1,000), HVAC tune-ups, and code-violation coverage.
  • Trade-off: the service fee ($100/$125) is among the highest, and monthly premiums run above average.
  • 30-day workmanship guarantee is short compared with Cinch (180 days) and Liberty Home Guard (365 days).
  • You cannot choose your own contractor.
Plain-English verdict: If your nightmare scenario is a $7,000 HVAC replacement, AHS's $5,000-per-system and $50,000 aggregate caps are the strongest in this lineup. You pay for it monthly and at every claim. Read the per-plan limit table in the sample contract - caps differ by plan tier.
