Key exclusions

  • Pre-existing conditions (known or unknown)
  • Non-standard installations
  • Improper maintenance
  • not available in Washington state

Waiting period

30 days after the agreement fee is received; immediate with proof of prior continuous coverage

Cancellation policy

Within 30 days of order date: full refund of agreement fee minus service costs. After 30 days: pro-rated refund for unexpired term, minus service costs and an admin fee (up to $50)

Fine-print highlights

  • Simple two-plan structure - both plans bundle systems AND appliances, no systems-only tier.
  • Flat $3,000-per-item cap keeps the math simple but can leave you short on big HVAC jobs.
  • 13-month contract term (one bonus month vs. the standard 12).
  • Single flat service fee - no tiered fee choice to game the monthly price.
  • $2.50/month surcharge reported for choosing monthly billing over annual.
  • 90-day parts / 30-day labor workmanship guarantee.
Plain-English verdict: Choice is the value pick for standard homes with mid-range systems: honest pricing, both plans cover systems and appliances, and the $3,000 cap is predictable. The catch is the pre-existing condition exclusion - document your equipment's condition at sign-up.
