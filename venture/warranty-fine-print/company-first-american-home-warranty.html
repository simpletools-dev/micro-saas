Fine-print highlights

  • The First American Advantage upgrade covers improper installations, modifications, and unknown pre-existing conditions - exclusions that sink most competitors' claims.
  • Premium plan's $7,000-per-appliance cap is the highest appliance cap we track.
  • Only available in 36 states - check availability before anything else.
  • Fewer add-ons than competitors, but add-ons are cheap (from ~$2/mo).
  • Monthly billing carries a $2 per-payment handling fee.
Plain-English verdict: If your appliances are the expensive part of your home (luxury kitchen, laundry), First American's uncapped systems and $7,000 appliance limit are hard to beat. Add the Advantage upgrade if your home has older or DIY-modified systems.
