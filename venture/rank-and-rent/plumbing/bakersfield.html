Serving Bakersfield

From Downtown and Westchester to the Southwest and East Bakersfield, we serve homes across the entire city — roughly 400,000 residents spread along the Kern River, Highway 99, and Highway 58.

Homes here

Bakersfield's housing stock runs the full range: 1920s–1950s bungalows and cottages near Downtown, Chester Avenue, and Westchester; mid-century ranch homes through the central neighborhoods; and large newer tract developments across the Southwest (Seven Oaks, Laurelglen, Panama Lane corridors) built from the 1990s onward. Each era brings its own service patterns — older homes with aging infrastructure, newer ones with builder-grade equipment hitting its first replacement cycle.

What we see most in this area

  • Kern County's hard water hits water heaters hard — annual flushing adds years of life, especially on 10+ year old tanks common in Oildale and Greenfield.
  • Older neighborhoods (Oildale, central Greenfield, East Bakersfield) still have galvanized supply lines and cast-iron sewer laterals — expect corrosion and root intrusion.
  • Slab foundations dominate here, so we use electronic leak detection before any concrete is touched.

Nearby areas we also serve

Bakersfield (citywide), Oildale, Rosedale, Greenfield — plus surrounding Kern County communities including Stockdale, Fruitvale, Seven Oaks, and Laurelglen.
