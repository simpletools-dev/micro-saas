Serving Oildale

Oildale sits just north of the Kern River — a long-established community with deep oilfield roots (the name says it all), centered on North Chester Avenue and Roberts Lane.

Homes here

Oildale has a high concentration of mid-century and older homes — many built from the 1940s through the 1970s. That means original-era infrastructure is common: aging water heaters, older electrical panels, cast-iron or galvanized plumbing, and garage doors and HVAC systems that have seen decades of Bakersfield summers. Repairs here often uncover deferred maintenance, so honest diagnosis matters more than a quick patch.

What we see most in this area

  • Kern County's hard water hits water heaters hard — annual flushing adds years of life, especially on 10+ year old tanks common in Oildale and Greenfield.
  • Older neighborhoods (Oildale, central Greenfield, East Bakersfield) still have galvanized supply lines and cast-iron sewer laterals — expect corrosion and root intrusion.
  • Slab foundations dominate here, so we use electronic leak detection before any concrete is touched.

Nearby areas we also serve

Bakersfield (citywide), Oildale, Rosedale, Greenfield — plus surrounding Kern County communities including Stockdale, Fruitvale, Seven Oaks, and Laurelglen.
