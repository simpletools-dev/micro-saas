What counts as an emergency

  • Door stuck open — your home is exposed; this is our top priority, especially after dark.
  • Car trapped in the garage — most openers have a manual release cord; if the door itself is jammed or off track, don't force it.
  • Broken spring with the door halfway — a half-open door can slam down. Keep people and pets clear.
  • Door off its track or hanging crooked — continued use bends the track further and risks the door falling.
  • Snapped cable — the door may drop suddenly on one side. Don't try to lift it alone.
Do NOT attempt to fix torsion springs yourself. They store enough energy to cause serious injury. Pulling the red manual-release cord to operate the door by hand is fine — touching the spring hardware is not.

What happens when you call

  1. We triage by phone. Tell us what the door is doing; we'll tell you whether it's safe to wait or needs someone now, and give you an arrival window.
  2. Technician arrives with a stocked truck. Springs, cables, rollers, and common opener parts ride on the truck, so most emergencies are fixed in one visit.
  3. Upfront price before work. Even on emergency calls, you approve the written price first.
  4. Full safety test. We cycle the door, check the auto-reverse, and make sure the manual release works before leaving.

While you wait

  • Keep the area under the door clear — don't park under a door that's acting up.
  • If the door is stuck open and you must leave, lock the interior door to the garage and secure valuables.
  • Don't keep pressing the opener button on a jammed door — it can strip gears or overheat the motor.

Common questions

Is there an extra charge for emergency calls?

After-hours and priority-dispatch calls can carry a service fee on top of the repair. We'll tell you exactly what it is on the phone before dispatching — no surprises.

Can I manually open a door with a broken spring?

Sometimes, with help — but it's heavy (often 150+ lbs of effective weight) and dangerous if the door is damaged. We recommend waiting for the technician.

How fast can someone get here?

It depends on the time of day and where our trucks are, but emergency calls jump the queue. Call and we'll give you an honest arrival window.
