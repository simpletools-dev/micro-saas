What we fix

Most garage door problems come down to a handful of worn or broken parts. Here's what we handle every day:

Broken springs

Torsion and extension springs do the heavy lifting. When one snaps, the door gets heavy or won't open at all — the single most common repair call we get.

Cables & rollers

Frayed lift cables, cables jumped off the drum, and worn steel or nylon rollers that make the door grind, stick, or hang crooked.

Openers & remotes

Dead openers, stripped gears, dead remotes and keypads, and safety sensors that won't align — plus full opener replacement when repair stops making sense.

Tracks & panels

Bent or misaligned tracks, damaged sections and panels, and doors knocked off track by a vehicle bump — straightened, replaced, or re-hung.

How a repair visit works

  1. You call or request a quote. Tell us what the door is doing (or not doing). We'll set an arrival window the same day when possible.
  2. Diagnosis on site. The technician inspects the springs, cables, rollers, tracks, and opener, then explains exactly what's wrong.
  3. Upfront price. You get a written price before any work starts — no surprises, no pressure.
  4. Repair and test. Most repairs are finished in under two hours, and the door is tested through full open/close cycles before we leave.
Safety note: garage door springs are under extreme tension. A broken torsion spring should never be handled by a homeowner — it's the one repair where a mistake can cause serious injury. Call a technician.

Common questions

Why won't my garage door open all the way?

The most common causes are a broken torsion spring, a misaligned safety sensor, or a stripped gear in the opener. A quick on-site diagnosis will pin it down — most of these are same-visit fixes.

How much does garage door repair cost?

It depends on the part and the door size. Simple fixes like sensor realignment or roller replacement are on the low end; spring replacement and new openers cost more. You always get a written quote before work begins, so there are no surprises.

Should I repair or replace my garage door?

If the door is structurally sound and under about 15 years old, repair is usually the smart money. Replacement makes sense when multiple panels are damaged, the door is warped, or you're paying for repeated repairs on an aging door.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes — same-day appointments are available across our service area, and emergency calls for doors stuck open or closed are prioritized.
