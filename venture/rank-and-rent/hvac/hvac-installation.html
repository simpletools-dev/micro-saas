Getting the size right

The most important part of an install isn't the brand — it's the sizing. An oversized AC short-cycles: it cools fast but never runs long enough to dehumidify or distribute air evenly, and it wears out faster. An undersized one runs all August afternoon and still loses. We run a Manual J load calculation (room sizes, insulation, windows, orientation) instead of guessing by square footage. If a contractor quotes you a size without measuring anything, that's a red flag.

What to know about efficiency ratings

SEER2 (cooling)

Higher = less electricity per unit of cooling. In Bakersfield, where the AC runs 6+ months a year, jumping from minimum efficiency to 16–17 SEER2 typically pays back in a few years of summer electric bills. Ask us to run the numbers for your usage.

AFUE (heating)

Measures furnace efficiency. Our mild winters mean heating payback is slower here — an 80% furnace is often fine, though 95%+ models exist if you want maximum efficiency.

What a complete quote includes

  • Equipment — exact condenser, coil/air-handler, and furnace model numbers
  • Labor and permits — Bakersfield/Kern County permits and inspections included
  • Thermostat — at minimum a modern programmable; smart thermostats are worth discussing
  • Ductwork assessment — we inspect your ducts and quote any needed repairs; new equipment on bad ducts is money wasted
  • Line set, pad, and electrical — new refrigerant lines, condenser pad, and any electrical upgrades, itemized
  • Haul-away — removal of the old system
  • Warranties — equipment warranty (registered) plus our labor warranty, in writing

Questions to ask before you sign

  • Did you do a Manual J load calculation? Can I see it?
  • What are the exact model numbers, SEER2, and AFUE?
  • Is the equipment warranty registered, and for how many years?
  • What's your labor warranty, and who do I call if something's wrong in year two?
  • Will you test airflow and refrigerant charge after install and show me the readings?
  • Are permits, inspections, and haul-away all included?

Common questions

How long does installation take?

A straightforward changeout is usually one day. Jobs involving ductwork modifications, electrical upgrades, or difficult access can run two days.

What time of year is cheapest to install?

Fall and winter — contractors are less slammed than in July. You may also find better equipment availability and scheduling flexibility.

Should I replace the furnace and AC together?

If both are old, yes — matched systems run more efficiently, you pay one labor mobilization, and you avoid the furnace failing two years after the AC was replaced.
