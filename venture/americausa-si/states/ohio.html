Why this state sits here

Ohio sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 2 verified announced AI data center projects and 1 chip fab project tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include AWS, SoftBank / Stargate partners, Intel.

Notable announced projects include the AWS New Albany data centers (AWS) in New Albany, reported at $6.2 billion (since 2015); the Stargate Lordstown (SoftBank / Stargate partners) in Lordstown, reported at undisclosed (part of Stargate program); the Intel chip fab (New Albany (Ohio One)) (Intel) in New Albany (Ohio One), reported at $28 billion (first phase).

Reported project statuses include: announced September 2025; operational (expanding); under construction (delayed to 2030/2031).
