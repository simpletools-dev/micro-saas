Why this state sits here

South Carolina sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 3 verified announced AI data center projects and 0 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include Google, Meta.

Notable announced projects include the Google Dorchester County data center (Google) in Dorchester County, reported at $3.3 billion (announced September 26, 2024); the Google South Carolina expansion (Google) in Dorchester County, reported at $9 billion over two years (announced October 13, 2025).
