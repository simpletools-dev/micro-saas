Why this state sits here

Oregon sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 2 verified announced AI data center projects and 1 chip fab project tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include Meta, AWS, Intel.

Notable announced projects include the Meta Prineville data center (Meta) in Prineville, reported at $2 billion+ (11-building campus, nearly 4.6M sq ft); the AWS eastern Oregon data centers (AWS) in Boardman / Umatilla / Hermiston, reported at $54.8 billion invested (39 data centers; 1,000+ more acres purchased near Boardman); the Intel chip fab (Hillsboro (D1X)) (Intel) in Hillsboro (D1X), reported at $3 billion (D1X expansion; $52 billion+ total Oregon investment).

Reported project statuses include: operational (expanding); operational (expansion opened).
