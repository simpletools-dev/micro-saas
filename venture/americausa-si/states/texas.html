Why this state sits here

Texas sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 3 verified announced AI data center projects and 3 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include OpenAI / Oracle / SoftBank / MGX (developed by Crusoe), OpenAI / Stargate partners, Meta, Samsung, Texas Instruments, SpaceX / Tesla / xAI (Terafab).

Notable announced projects include the Stargate Abilene (flagship campus) (OpenAI / Oracle / SoftBank / MGX (developed by Crusoe)) in Abilene, reported at $500 billion (Stargate program total; Abilene is the 1.2 GW flagship campus); the Stargate Shackelford County and Milam County sites (OpenAI / Stargate partners) in Shackelford County and Milam County, reported at undisclosed (part of Stargate program); the Samsung chip fab (Taylor) (Samsung) in Taylor, reported at $37 billion (two fabs, planned).

Reported project statuses include: announced (expected launch 2028); announced September 2025; operational (expanding); proposed; under construction (delayed); under construction (first buildings operational).
