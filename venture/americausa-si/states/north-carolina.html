Why this state sits here

North Carolina sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 2 verified announced AI data center projects and 1 chip fab project tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include AWS, Nscale (with WhiteFiber), Wolfspeed.

Notable announced projects include the AWS Richmond County data center complex (AWS) in Richmond County, reported at $10 billion (announced June 2025); the Nscale / WhiteFiber NC-1 AI data center (Nscale (with WhiteFiber)) in Madison, reported at $865 million (10-year, 40 MW colocation agreement); the Wolfspeed chip fab (Siler City (Chatham County)) (Wolfspeed) in Siler City (Chatham County), reported at $5 billion (silicon carbide).

Reported project statuses include: announced December 2025; nearing production readiness (late 2025); under construction (broke ground October 31, 2025; 20 buildings planned).
