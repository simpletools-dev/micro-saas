Why this state sits here

Our research found no major announced AI data center or chip fab projects in Washington as of October 2026, which is why the state sits in the Watching tier. That is a statement about announced projects only. It is not a judgment on the state's technology sector, workforce, or future prospects: several states with deep tech economies have little announced buildout so far, often because of high power prices, limited large sites, or other siting factors.

General context: what the buildout wave looks for

This is general background about AI campus siting, not a claim about Washington.

Developers of large AI data centers typically weigh a short list of factors: low industrial electricity prices with available grid capacity, large affordable sites near fiber routes, predictable permitting and local support, and water or advanced cooling options. Chip fabs add their own demands: extremely stable power, ultrapure water supplies, and specialized workforce pipelines.

What to watch: new project announcements, utility interconnection filings, or state incentive packages are the signals that would move a Watching state up the tiers in a future update.
