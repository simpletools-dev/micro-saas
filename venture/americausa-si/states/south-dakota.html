Why this state sits here

South Dakota sits in the Emerging tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 2 verified announced AI data center projects and 0 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States showing early signs of buildout: one or two announced projects, with the footprint still small next to the leading states.

Companies with announced projects in the state include Gemini Capital (Gemini Data Center SD), Applied Digital.

Notable announced projects include the Gemini Capital Sioux Falls Technology Park (Gemini Capital (Gemini Data Center SD)) in Sioux Falls, reported at undisclosed (160 acres; 500 MW power target); the Applied Digital Deuel County data center (Applied Digital) in Toronto (Deuel County), reported at multi-billion-dollar (up to 430 MW proposed).

Reported project statuses include: early stage (annexation and rezoning approved 2025-2026); proposed.
