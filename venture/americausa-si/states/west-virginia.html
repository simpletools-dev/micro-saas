Why this state sits here

West Virginia sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 3 verified announced AI data center projects and 0 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include Nscale, Google, Penzance Management.

Notable announced projects include the Nscale Monarch Compute Campus (Nscale) in Mason County, reported at undisclosed (up to 8 GW planned; full campus projected at about $71 billion per planning documents); the Google Putnam County data center campus (Google) in Putnam County, reported at multi-billion-dollar.

Reported project statuses include: early stages (land purchased March 2026; approved as High Impact Development Project); in development (acquired March 2026; first phase 2 GW online by 2028; Anthropic signed 460 MW agreement); planning / pre-construction.
