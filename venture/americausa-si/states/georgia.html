Why this state sits here

Georgia sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 3 verified announced AI data center projects and 0 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include Meta, AWS, CleanSpark (tenant: Meta).

Notable announced projects include the Meta Stanton Springs data center (Meta) in Newton County (Stanton Springs), reported at $2 billion+; the AWS Georgia data centers (AWS) in undisclosed locations, reported at $11 billion (announced January 2025).

Reported project statuses include: announced; operational since 2018; under construction (175 MW delivery late 2027).
