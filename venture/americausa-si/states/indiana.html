Why this state sits here

Indiana sits in the Leader tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 4 verified announced AI data center projects and 0 chip fab projects tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with the largest concentration of announced AI data center and chip fab projects: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.

Companies with announced projects in the state include AWS, Google, Meta.

Notable announced projects include the AWS New Carlisle campus (Project Rainier) (AWS) in New Carlisle (St. Joseph County), reported at $11 billion; the AWS Indiana expansion (AWS) in undisclosed locations, reported at $15 billion additional (announced November 2025).

Reported project statuses include: announced; completed October 2025 (30 buildings, 2.2 GW; built to train Anthropic's Claude models); operational December 2025 (expanding); under construction.
