Why this state sits here

New Mexico sits in the Builder tier of our SI readiness ranking, based on 1 verified announced AI data center project and 1 chip fab project tracked in our database as of October 2026. States with a meaningful announced footprint: at least one significant campus or fab, ahead of most of the country but below the largest hubs.

Companies with announced projects in the state include OpenAI / Stargate partners, Intel.

Notable announced projects include the Stargate Dona Ana County (Project Jupiter) (OpenAI / Stargate partners) in Dona Ana County, reported at undisclosed (part of Stargate program); the Intel chip fab (Rio Rancho) (Intel) in Rio Rancho, reported at $3.5 billion (announced May 2021).

Reported project statuses include: announced September 2025; under construction.
