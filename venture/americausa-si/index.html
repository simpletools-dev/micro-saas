Methodology

How the SI readiness tiers work

Tiers are an editorial ranking of announced projects as of October 2026, not a prediction of outcomes. Announced does not mean built: projects can stall, shrink, or be canceled.

  • Leader The largest concentration of announced buildout: multiple major campuses, headline multi-billion dollar commitments, or both.
  • Builder A meaningful announced footprint: at least one significant campus or fab, ahead of most of the country but below the largest hubs.
  • Emerging Early signs of buildout: one or two announced projects, with the footprint still small next to the leading states.
  • Watching No major announced AI data center or chip fab projects found as of October 2026. This reflects announcements only, not the state's tech economy or future.
