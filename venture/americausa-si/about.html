About AmericaUSA.si

The SI Era, State by State: an independent tracker of where America is building superintelligence.

What this site is

AmericaUSA.si maps the US superintelligence buildout across all 50 states: announced AI data centers, chip fabrication plants, and our SI readiness ranking of each state. Every project is cited to a source. Every state page stands on its own, whether the state hosts a dozen campuses or none yet.

Methodology

We compiled 71 verified announced AI data center projects and 17 chip fab projects from press coverage and company announcements, each linked to its source on the relevant state page. Population figures come from the US Census Bureau 2024 estimates; industrial power prices come from the US Energy Information Administration's Electric Power Monthly (July 2025). States were sorted into four SI readiness tiers (Leader, Builder, Emerging, Watching) based on the count, scale, and status of their announced projects. See the tiers explainer for the full methodology.

Honest limitations

  • Announced projects, not completions. A project on this site may be delayed, downsized, or canceled after announcement.
  • Figures are reported claims. Investment amounts and job projections come from company statements and press coverage, cited per project. We did not independently audit them.
  • Tiers are an editorial ranking of announcements. They describe where announced buildout stands as of October 2026, not where it will end up.
  • Coverage is finite. Smaller facilities and unannounced projects are outside the count.
