SI Readiness Rankings

All 50 states, ranked by announced AI data center and chip fab buildout as of October 2026. 15 Leaders, 20 Builders, 8 Emerging, 7 Watching.

Methodology: how tiers were assigned

We counted verified announced AI data center projects and chip fab projects in each state, weighing the number of projects, their reported scale, and their status (operational, under construction, or announced). States with the largest concentration of major announcements became Leaders; states with a smaller but real footprint became Builders; states with one or two early projects became Emerging; states where we found no major announced projects became Watching.

An honest note on what these rankings are

These tiers are an editorial ranking of announcements, not a scorecard of results. An announced project is not a completed one: campuses get delayed, downsized, or canceled, and reported investment figures come from company statements and press coverage, which we cite project by project. A state's tier can change as new projects are announced. The tiers say nothing definitive about where the buildout will end up, only where the announcements stand as of October 2026.
