How the estimate works

For each ZIP we take the area's average sunshine (NREL PVWatts, kWh/m²/day), size a system to cover a typical California home's 6,500 kWh/year of use, and value its production at the current EIA California residential rate of 33.61¢/kWh (July 2026). The 25-year figure is a range — flat rates on the low end, 2.5%/year rate growth on the high end — before system cost and incentives. Every ZIP page lists its full assumptions.
